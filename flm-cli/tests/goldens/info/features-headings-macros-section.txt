Macro \section

macro \section

\section*[options]{title}\label{target}

\section*[…]{title}\label{…}: A heading of level 1.

Arguments

* star: A star: not numbered (numbered=false).

[options] options: Key-values: numbered=true|false, toc=true|false.

{title} title: The heading’s title.

Fields written after it

\label{target} label: Attaches a label to the construct, which a reference can
point at; may be repeated. It may be written more than once.

{target} target (flm.refs.defined-label): The label, written type:label
(sec:intro). (an argument of the field \label)

Declared by: the feature headings <flm info features/headings>

Available in: text mode

Element: flm.heading <flm info namespaces/element/flm.heading>: section: A
heading of level 1.

Aspects: flm_core::model::aspects::BlockRoleAspect,
flm_core::model::aspects::Sectioning, flm_core::model::aspects::Referenceable
