A single backtick: x. Two backticks around a single one: a`b. Three around two: a``b.

One space at each end is removed: `a` holds a backtick at each end, and x holds x alone. A space at one end only is kept: x. A span of spaces only is kept: .

A backslash and a percent sign are raw characters: \emph{x} % y, and so are braces: { and }.

A span may be wrapped: over two lines, and x has a line ending at each end.
