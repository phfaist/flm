macro \section

\section*[short]{title}

Starts a section of the document, with a title written as inline text.

A second paragraph of the body: the section’s number appears in \ref.

Arguments

* star: The section is not numbered.

[short] short: The title shown in the table of contents.

{title} title (flm.headings.title): The title.

<label>

{body}

The first paragraph of a longer text.

Its second paragraph, with code.

Fields

\label{…} label (flm.refs.defined-label): The label a reference points at.

Declared by: the feature headings <flm info features/headings>

Element: flm.headings.heading

Available in: text
